This design proposal for Amabo Joshua’s portfolio leverages the power of Next.js
to create a high-performance, aesthetically refined digital experience. The
design philosophy is "Architectural Minimalism"—blending the structured
discipline of a Computer Engineer with the "chic" elegance of a modern creative
professional.

1. The Color Palette & Aesthetic Foundation

We will use a high-contrast but muted palette to evoke a premium "Editorial"
feel:

  - Base: Soft Cream (Background) – Provides a warmer, more sophisticated feel
    than pure white.
  - Typography: Deep Charcoal – High readability and authority.
  - Accents: Burgundy (Action items, highlights) and Sage Green (Technical
    badges, success states).
  - Structural Elements: Light Gray (Dividers, subtle shadows).

2. The Navigation: "The Glass Pill"

Since the client requested a floating navbar, we will design a "Dock-style"
navigation bar centered at the bottom or top of the viewport.

  - Appearance: A semi-transparent "Frosted Glass" (Glassmorphism) pill-shaped
    container using the Light Gray and Soft Cream blur.
  - Interaction: As the user scrolls, the navbar shrinks slightly.
  - Gestures: Instead of a standard click, the icons/labels have a Magnetic
    Effect. When the cursor nears an item, the element subtly leans towards the
    cursor. The transitions between sections use a Cross-Dissolve and
    Slide—where the new content feels like it's sliding into place on a silk
    track.

3. Section-by-Section Layout

I. The Hero Section (The Impression)

  - Layout: Split-screen or centered minimalist typography.
  - Text: A massive, sophisticated serif font for "AMABO JOSHUA," contrasted by
    a clean, monospace sans-serif for "(Computer Engineer)."
  - Motion: A subtle Parallax Mouse Effect where a faint "Sage Green" geometric
    wireframe (representing engineering) moves slightly behind the text.
  - CTA: A Burgundy "View Work" button that expands slightly on hover.

II. The "Tech Stack" Marquee

  - Visual: Below the hero, a slow-moving, infinite horizontal scroll of his
    tech stack (React Native, Nest.js, Django, Flutter).
  - Chic Touch: The text is outlined (hollow) and only fills with Sage Green
    color when the user scrolls over it.

III. Featured Projects (The Gallery)

Instead of a standard grid, we use a "Staggered Editorial Grid".

  - iChef: A large feature card. Since it's a "Living Culinary Archive," the
    background of the card could have a very subtle, desaturated video loop of
    traditional textures.
  - Rekom-n: A mobile-mockup display. A high-fidelity 3D render of a phone
    "floating" in the Soft Cream space.
  - Interaction: On hover, the project cards should not "pop" (which is
    childish); instead, they should slowly scale up (1.02x) and the background
    color should shift from Soft Cream to a very faint Light Gray to create
    depth.

IV. Professional Experience (The Timeline)

  - Visual: A thin, vertical Burgundy line runs down the center.
  - Content: As the user scrolls, the experience points (Seven Common Factor,
    QTA Tech) fade in from the left and right.
  - Gesture: "Scroll-Triggered Reveal." The text doesn't just appear; it
    "unmasks" from top to bottom, mimicking the way code is written or read.

V. Awards & Certifications (The Grid)

  - Layout: A clean 3-column grid using "Bento Box" styling. Each box (HCIA
    Datacom, IBM Design Thinking) has a subtle border.
  - Detail: When clicked, a small side-drawer (not a popup) slides out from the
    right to show the Credly/LinkedIn verification details.

4. Professional "Gestures" & Micro-interactions

To ensure the site feels "Chic" and not "Childish":

  - Smooth Scroll: Implementation of Lenis Scroll or Framer Motion to give the
    page a "weighty," premium scrolling feel.
  - Cursor Follower: A small, Deep Charcoal ring that follows the cursor. When
    it hovers over a clickable link, the ring expands and turns into a Soft
    Burgundy glow.
  - Page Transitions: When navigating between "Home" and a "Project Detail"
    page, use a Shared Layout Animation where the project image physically moves
    and expands to become the header of the next page. This creates a "seamless
    flow" rather than a "loading jump."

5. Technical Stack for Implementation

  - Framework: Next.js 14+ (App Router) for lightning-fast server-side
    rendering.
  - Styling: Tailwind CSS (for the Soft Cream/Burgundy/Sage Green utility
    classes).
  - Animations: Framer Motion for the sophisticated gestures.
  - Typography:
      - Header: 'Playfair Display' or 'Cormorant Garamond' (Chic/Classy).
      - Body/Code: 'Inter' or 'JetBrains Mono' (Professional/Engineering).

Summary of the "Vibe"

The site will feel like walking through a high-end modern art gallery dedicated
to software architecture. It uses the Soft Cream to feel inviting, the Burgundy
to show passion and leadership, and the Sage Green to highlight technical
growth. The floating navbar serves as the anchor, ensuring the "Computer
Engineer" is always just one refined click away.
